# Pizzazz artwork

These assets are stored locally and work offline. Food photography is AI-generated illustrative imagery, not official Pizza Hut product photography.

- `pizza-hero.png`: welcome image and home banner. Built-in image generation tool.
- `menu-photography.png`: a 2-column × 4-row photo atlas. CSS displays one cell per menu item: Margherita, Pepperoni, Veggie, BBQ Chicken, Creamy Pasta, Wings, Fries, Cola. Built-in image generation tool.
- `pizzazz-mark.svg`: original scalable slice-and-spark mark, matching the existing SVG interface approach. Used for app identity and favicon.

## Generation prompts

Hero: “Use case: product-mockup. Create a premium realistic food photograph for a pizza ordering mobile app. Square image. One whole freshly baked pepperoni pizza, overhead view, centered and entirely visible with generous 10 percent empty margin around it. Beautiful golden puffed crust, melted mozzarella, deep red crispy pepperoni, tiny fresh basil leaves. Warm ivory plain background, soft natural studio light and subtle shadow. Appetizing commercial food photography, crisp natural texture, no plate, no hands, no text, no logos, no watermark.”

Menu: “Create a food photography contact sheet for a restaurant app, exactly 2 columns by 4 rows, eight equally sized square cells, no gutters, no borders, no text or labels. Every cell has the same plain warm ivory background and one centered entire food item with generous margin. Photorealistic premium studio overhead food photography, appetizing real textures. Strict cell positions: row 1 left whole margherita pizza with melted mozzarella tomato and basil, row 1 right whole pepperoni pizza; row 2 left whole vegetarian pizza with green peppers mushrooms onions and black olives, row 2 right whole BBQ chicken pizza with grilled chicken onions and smoky sauce; row 3 left a white shallow bowl of creamy penne pasta with herbs, row 3 right six golden glazed chicken wings on a small ivory plate; row 4 left crispy golden french fries in a small ivory bowl, row 4 right a single glass of iced cola with condensation. All dishes fully inside their own cell. No hands, cutlery, logos, watermark or typography. Portrait image ratio 1:2.”

## App logo

`pizzazz-logo.png` is an unchanged copy of the user-provided `Glossy Pizza P Icon.png`. Used for the welcome and Home brand marks, browser favicon, and Apple touch icon. The older SVG is retained as an unused legacy asset.
